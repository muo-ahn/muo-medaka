## JMA (日本メダカ協会): can the manual be read online?

- The manual page https://jma-medaka.com/medaka-variety-classification-manual/ says it is **free to download and use**. Nothing was bought.
  - The **5th edition (2025-09)** full manual is free to read: `250901_【公式】品種分類マニュアル_第５版.pdf`, 84 pages, 9.3 MB. The text can be extracted, but only with `-enc UTF-8`; the default encoding drops all the Japanese.
  - 形質一覧表（第５版）_250901.pdf is a one-page trait table and is readable.
  - 2020nixtukune-mu.pdf is the nickname→品種名 早見表. It is dated H31.4.1 (2019), so it is older than the 5th edition. `-table` mode keeps its columns aligned.
  - Also linked on that page but not read: 2020hinnsyuhayami.pdf, 250901_公式品種分類マニュアル2025_追加事項のみ.pdf, and 形質一覧表2024（めだかの館カタログより）.pdf.
  - A printed edition is sold separately (jma3.shop-pro.jp, Yahoo shop). It was not bought and is not needed.
- The manual is the main source for trait (形質) definitions, 形質補足, 共通補足, the naming rules, and the オーロラ / 非透明鱗 / 錦・更紗・斑 explanations.
