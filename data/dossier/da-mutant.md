# Da mutant

BODY_SHAPE

Laboratory medaka mutant showing mirror-image duplication of the ventral half across the lateral midline. Caused by a transposon insertion in the zic1/zic4 enhancer.

## Aliases

- Double anal fin
- Da
- double-anal-fin mutant

## Phenotypes

- dorsal-to-ventral identity transformation
